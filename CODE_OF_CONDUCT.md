# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to keep participation respectful, technical and safe. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing and disclosure of private data are not tolerated.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Use fictional data in examples and bug reports.
- Respect confidentiality: do not post credentials, customer data or internal documents.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and security disclosure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, threats or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing vulnerabilities, backdoors or hidden data collection.
- Using the project to target real systems without authorization.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports can be sent through the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when confidentiality is required.

This document is a short project-specific code of conduct, not a legal instrument.
